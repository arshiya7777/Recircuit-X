## 🌟 Project Overview
**ReCircuit X** is a full-stack enterprise-grade web application designed to bridge the gap between consumer electronics disposal, certified recycling facilities, and underprivileged educational NGOs. The platform facilitates a sustainable circular economy by integrating automated device valuation, secure logistics dispatch, and cryptographic data erasure compliance.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Modules

### 🛍️ 1. Live Marketplace & Inventory (52+ Items)
* Fully stocked with 52+ verified items (Smartphones, Laptops, Smart TVs).
* Advanced filtering by Category (*Smartphones, Laptops, Televisions*) and Action Route (*Sell, Donate, Recycle*).

### ⚡ 2. Post & AI Valuation Engine
* Automated residual valuation based on brand specifications, physical condition, and age degradation curves.
* **Photo Upload & Condition Guide:** Interactive inspection wizard with clear criteria for screen scratches, dents, and battery health.

### 🚚 3. Enterprise Logistics & Live Tracking Hub
* Real-time milestone progress tracking for dispatches.
* **Equipment Diagnostics & Data Risk Advisory:** Automated warnings regarding active account tokens and cryptographic overwrites for each device type.
* Multi-role RBAC Portal (**End-User**, **NGO Organization**, **Certified Recycler**).

### 📊 4. Gamified Impact Dashboard
* **Eco-Tier System:** Tracks user progression from beginner to *Gold Circular Champion*.
* **Real-World CO₂ Equivalency:** Translates carbon offset into tangible metrics (e.g., equivalent trees planted, bike rides saved).
* **Resource Recovery Breakdown:** Visualizes exact precious metal extraction metrics (*Gold, Silver, Copper, Cobalt & Lithium*).
* **Interactive Ledger:** Clickable GreenCoins and Carbon offset cards linking to complete transaction history.

### 🛡️ 5. NIST SP 800-88 Data Sanitization & Certificates
* Regulatory compliance documentation explaining multi-pass binary overwriting and cryptographic erase (CE) protocols.
* **Past Certificates Archive & Live Preview Modal:** Instant access to verifiable cryptographic erasure certificates with working PDF downloads.

### 💸 6. Secure Escrow Wallet & AI Eco-Assistant
* Simulated instant UPI/Bank escrow payout for marketplace sellers.
* 24/7 floating **ReCircuit AI Support Chatbot** for instant user assistance on data wiping and recycling queries.
* **Regional Eco-Radar:** Live community and NGO dispatch ticker across the Mumbai & Navi Mumbai region.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS, Modern Responsive UI/UX Architecture.
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js RESTful APIs.
* **Compliance Standards:** NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 (Media Sanitization), CPCB E-Waste Guidelines.
